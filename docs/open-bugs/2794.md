## Two-process nullable-date handoff (2026-09-15)

The additional Shared-mode runner follows #1742's client/service handoff using
the five reported BSON date-field names. Two actual processes update the same
24 jobs across 24 rounds, alternating ownership through the deletion status.
Patterned payloads and Guid dictionaries grow and shrink across page boundaries.
Each attempt verifies 1,152 acknowledged updates, full typed ledgers, raw BSON
date names/types/values, a fresh-process reopen, and unchanged read-only bytes.

On Linux/.NET 8, both 5.0.7 and current source pass all **3/3 attempts**. This
extends the previous single-process control but still does not reproduce the
unknown originating corruption. The full customer model and storage/interruption
history remain unavailable.

An initial explicit-transaction variant also passed 5.0.7 in 3/3 attempts, but
timed out on dev, consistent with the mutex retention already covered by #2787.
That separate failure is retained in the evidence and is not counted as #2794.
The final handoff uses ordinary atomic `Update` calls with one owner per job.
